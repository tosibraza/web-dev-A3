01 — CIVIC TECHNOLOGY↗

GREENGOSMARTER WASTE · CLEANER COMMUNITIES
APP CONCEPT · WASTE MANAGEMENT

GreenGo — A smarter approach to waste management

GreenGo is a civic-focused application concept designed to make waste management more organised and community-driven. The idea is to help people understand waste segregation, stay informed about collection activity and report local waste-related issues through one digital platform.

The planned experience includes guidance for dry, wet and hazardous waste, garbage vehicle tracking, an issue-reporting feature, a community feed, local participation through a leaderboard and a reward concept called Green Coins. The broader goal is to encourage everyday participation in cleaner neighbourhoods.

The project explores how a mobile application, a database and AI-assisted classification ideas could work together. It is an evolving concept and a way for me to think about product design, user needs and technology for public benefit.

02 — AI EXPERIMENT↗
ACE_AIWASTE CLASSIFICATION CONCEPT
AI / IMAGE CLASSIFICATION

Ace — AI Trash Segregator

Ace is an AI-based concept connected to the GreenGo idea. Its purpose is to explore whether a camera-based system can help users identify the category of waste they are handling.

The concept focuses on image input and waste classification, with the aim of making segregation guidance easier to understand. It represents my interest in combining artificial intelligence with a practical environmental problem.

Working on this idea has encouraged me to think about datasets, image recognition, user experience and the importance of testing an AI system carefully before depending on its predictions.
